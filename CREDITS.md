## 3D models

The two wind tunnels' test sections (`public/models/tunnel-university.glb` and
`public/models/tunnel-own.glb`) are built by Longlight Interactive's own script,
`art/tunnel/build.py`, in Blender 4.5.15. The velodrome
(`public/models/velodrome.glb`), the ski-jump hill's buildings
(`public/models/skijump.glb`), the circuits' trackside (`public/models/circuit.glb`),
the salt flat's camp (`public/models/saltflat.glb`), Belyanka airfield's
buildings (`public/models/airfield.glb`), the sliding track's buildings
(`public/models/bobsled.glb`), the open road's trees, farms and finish
(`public/models/road.glb`), the drone club's meadow (`public/models/meadow.glb`)
the Ozimka survey fields (`public/models/fields.glb`), the Stavren Ice Oval
(`public/models/iceoval.glb`) and Lake Dolgo (`public/models/lake.glb`) are built the same way by `art/velodrome/build.py`, `art/skijump/build.py`,
`art/circuit/build.py`, `art/saltflat/build.py`, `art/airfield/build.py`,
`art/bobsled/build.py`, `art/road/build.py`, `art/meadow/build.py`,
`art/fields/build.py`, `art/iceoval/build.py` and `art/lake/build.py`, and
the people's smooth body parts (`public/models/body.glb`) by `art/body/build.py`.
Blender is GPL-licensed software; the models it outputs belong to the studio.

## Sound

The lab sounds are synthesized in code with the Web Audio API (`src/audio/`).

The 18 sound effects in `public/sfx/` (the tunnel's air and spin-up, the
turntable, a model snapping, the smoke, the model shop, the fee, a new brief,
and the bikes, crowd, skis, sleds, cars, trucks, planes and drones in the
replays) were generated by Longlight Interactive with ElevenLabs' sound-effect
generator (model `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`) on 17 November 2026, under the
studio's paid ElevenLabs plan, which allows commercial use. Two takes each; the
better one is kept, levelled to about -18 LUFS. The list is `src/data/sfx.ts`.
Until one has loaded, the code-made sound plays in its place.

## Music

The two music loops in `public/music/` (a calm one for the lab, an upbeat one
for the replays) were generated by Longlight Interactive with ElevenLabs Music
(model `eleven_music_v2_5`, instrumental) on 17 November 2026, under the
studio's paid ElevenLabs plan, which allows commercial use. Two takes each; the
steadier one is kept, levelled to about -18 LUFS. The list is `src/data/music.ts`.
